Riktig Mat

See what's really in your food

Riktig Mat scans a barcode and gives one honest number out of 100, weighted by how processed the food is, with better options from the same shelf.

Support

Found a bug, missing a product or have a question? Email johanstudt@gmail.com.

FAQ

Why is there no score? The ingredient list is missing from the database. Photograph it in the app and we will work out a score.

Where does the data come from? From Open Food Facts, an open database built by volunteers. You can correct a product there.

Can brands influence the score? No. The same maths applies to every product.
