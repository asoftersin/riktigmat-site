Privacy Policy for Riktig Mat

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Riktig Mat does not collect any personal data.

  • No account. You never need to sign up or log in.
  • History stays on your phone. What you scan is stored only on your device and is deleted when you clear the history or remove the app.
  • The camera is used only to read barcodes and ingredient lists. Images are processed on the phone and are not sent or stored.
  • Product lookups. When you scan an item, the barcode is sent to the open database Open Food Facts to fetch product data. No information about you is included. Open Food Facts handles requests under its own privacy policy.
  • No tracking, no ads and no analytics.

Questions? Contact us at johanstudt@gmail.com.
